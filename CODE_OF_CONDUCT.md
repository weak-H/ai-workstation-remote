# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community. Be considerate, assume good faith while evidence is incomplete, and
focus criticism on ideas, behavior, and observable results rather than people.

Expected behavior includes:

- welcoming people with different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting privacy, consent, and project security boundaries;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them without harassment;
- disclosing conflicts of interest that affect a contribution or review.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained
personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or
publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community
spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through a channel listed
by the repository owner. Do not open a public issue that exposes a target's
identity or private evidence. Maintainers will review reports in good faith,
protect reporter privacy where reasonably possible, and may edit or remove
content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants when
needed to protect the community.

Good-faith disagreement with a technical decision is not misconduct. Retaliation
against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
